BUSINESS DESCRIPTION — Driftline Coffee Roasters

Website URL: https://driftline.example
In their own words, what the business does: We roast specialty coffee in small batches out of one facility and sell it two ways — a direct-to-consumer subscription that ships every two or four weeks, and wholesale to independent cafés who put our coffee on their own menus. We started four years ago with me roasting on weekends and selling bags at the farmers market, and it grew into a real production operation faster than our process did. Right now we're at roughly $1.8M a year, split pretty evenly between the subscription side and wholesale, with a smaller slice from event and one-off retail sales. The subscription business runs fine — it's mostly automated through Shopify. Wholesale is where the strain is: we've got 26 café accounts and a waitlist of inbound leads we can't get through fast enough, because sampling and quoting still run through me personally instead of a documented process.
Main products / services: Subscription coffee bags (12oz, roasted-to-order, delivered every 2-4 weeks), wholesale roasted beans sold by the case to café accounts, seasonal single-origin retail bags, coffee merch (mugs, brew gear), occasional pop-up and farmers-market events.
Team size (rough): 8 (founder/CEO, head roaster, assistant roaster, ops & fulfillment lead, marketing, customer experience, 2 packers)
Anything unusual worth knowing: There is no dedicated wholesale owner — leads, samples, quotes, and reorders all route informally through the founder, who also personally roasts every wholesale sample batch between production runs. Wholesale pricing lives in a shared spreadsheet, not a standing price sheet. Production is still logged on paper at the roaster. Everything else (subscriptions, inventory, comms) runs through Shopify, QuickBooks, Slack, and Google Workspace.
