## Business Model Canvas

Driftline is a $1.8M small-batch roaster running a smooth, automated DTC subscription business alongside a wholesale café channel that generates the largest revenue share but is bottlenecked entirely by founder-run sampling, quoting, and reordering. The subscription engine funds the business reliably while wholesale growth (26 accounts, waitlist of inbound leads) is capped by throughput, not demand — a diagnosis corroborated by a lost deal, a churned account, and a cautious distributor pilot. Fixing the wholesale sample-to-quote and reorder process is the single highest-leverage move available to the business.
