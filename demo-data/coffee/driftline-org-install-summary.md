## The constraint we staffed against

THE constraint is a policy — all wholesale sampling, quoting, pricing, and reorder approval is restricted to one person (Maya), who is simultaneously producing — capping system throughput regardless of demand. A packing-capacity constraint-in-waiting sits behind it.

That sentence is signed. Everything below exists because of it. Brand awareness is in the record as your view. It is not what we staffed. A waitlist already exists. Demand is not the limiter.

Four desks. One ★ per desk. Agents draft. You and the stewards sign. No invented judge seat. Systems from intake only: Shopify, QuickBooks, Slack, Google Workspace. Pricing lives in the shared spreadsheet. There is no wholesale portal.
