ORG CHART — Driftline Coffee Roasters

Maya T. | Founder / CEO | reports to | board of one (self) | Approves all spend over $500; roasts every wholesale sample personally between production runs; is the de facto owner of wholesale sourcing, sample roasting, pricing, and new-account approval even though none of that is in her job title.

Jonah K. | Head Roaster | reports to | Maya T. | Production roasting schedule, green coffee sourcing relationships, quality of the roast itself. Does not touch wholesale sampling or quoting — that stays with Maya even though it competes for his production calendar.

Priya S. | Ops & Fulfillment | reports to | Maya T. | Pack/ship for subscriptions, case-packing for wholesale, inventory, carrier relationship, and — informally, without title or authority to change it — most of the day-to-day wholesale account relationship once an account exists (reorders, delivery issues, churn conversations). Owns the operational reality of a function she doesn't formally own.

Deb | Assistant Roaster | reports to | Jonah K. | Supports production roasting, packaging runs, equipment maintenance.

Rae | Marketing | reports to | Maya T. | Subscription marketing, social content, email campaigns. Not involved in wholesale marketing or lead gen — wholesale leads arrive inbound and get routed straight to Maya.

Sam | Customer Experience | reports to | Priya S. | Subscriber support, order issues, some wholesale account touchpoints when Priya delegates them. Closest thing to a wholesale relationship owner in practice, but it isn't a formal assignment.

Packers ×2 | Fulfillment staff | reports to | Priya S. | Pack and ship subscription orders and wholesale cases; capacity is effectively maxed during wholesale reorder weeks, which is the first place wholesale growth would break physically even if the sales process were fixed.

Where do decisions stall most: Anything wholesale. New leads, sample requests, pricing quotes, and reorder approvals all route through Maya personally because there is no assigned wholesale owner — Jonah owns roasting, Priya owns fulfillment, Sam touches accounts informally, but no one has the authority or bandwidth to own the sample-to-quote process end to end. The result is a sample-to-first-order lag averaging over a week, and existing accounts reordering by texting Maya directly rather than through any system. Every wholesale decision of consequence waits for the one person who is also personally roasting every sample.
