ORG CHART — Ironwood Door Co.

Walt Vance | Founder & Chairman (semi-retired) | reports to: nobody / advisory only | brand relationships, occasional big legacy-customer visits, informal QC spot-checks | comes in Tuesdays and Thursdays, still takes calls from customers who ask for him by name, sometimes reorders the day's schedule on his own without telling Carla

Derek Vance | Owner / General Manager | reports to: himself (family ownership, informal board of one) | overall strategy, marketing budget and vendor decisions, supplier contracts, final hiring approval, P&L | wants to push spend into ads and brand; defers almost all day-to-day operating decisions to Carla but hasn't given her budget or headcount to fix the phone problem she keeps raising

Carla Nunez | Office Manager | reports to: Derek Vance | inbound phones, scheduling/dispatch for all techs and install crew, permit filings, payroll, accounts payable/receivable, HR paperwork and onboarding | this is where decisions stall — she owns five distinct functions alone, has no backup for scheduling, and any change to phone coverage or staffing needs Derek's sign-off, which is slow because he's focused on marketing spend instead

Priya Sandoval | Customer Service Rep / Scheduler | reports to: Carla Nunez | answering inbound calls, booking service jobs, confirming appointment windows, texting job/arrival updates | primary daytime phone coverage; when she's on one line, the next call rolls to voicemail

Jesse Byrne | Customer Service Rep / Scheduler | reports to: Carla Nunez | overflow call coverage, voicemail triage and callbacks, booking, light AR follow-up | covers Priya's gaps but is often pulled into permit paperwork by Carla, which pulls him off phones during peak hours

Nate Ferris | Install Sales & Operations Manager | reports to: Derek Vance | install quoting, in-home measures, install crew scheduling, project sales | often the bottleneck on quote turnaround — he also runs the install crew day-to-day, so quoting competes with active job supervision; no dedicated estimator

Install crew ×4 | Installer | reports to: Nate Ferris | door and opener installation, site prep, haul-away of old doors, punch-list/walkthrough | one crew covers the whole territory; no capacity to run two simultaneous install jobs, which caps how many installs Nate can even schedule

Marcus Whitfield | Lead Service Technician | reports to: Carla Nunez (dispatch) / informally mentors under Walt Vance (technical) | technical escalations, training new techs, quality checks on repairs, sometimes covers Nate's install quotes when Nate is on a job | most senior tech, been with the company 17 years, customers request him by name which further concentrates scheduling pressure on Carla

Service techs ×7 | Service Technician | reports to: Carla Nunez (dispatch) | diagnosing and repairing springs, cables, tracks, rollers, and openers on scheduled and same-day emergency calls; on-site upsell of maintenance plans | fully booked most days in-season; no formal after-hours or weekend rotation, so evening/weekend calls default to voicemail

Ruth Okafor | Warehouse & Parts Manager | reports to: Derek Vance | inventory control, ordering springs/openers/panels/hardware, truck stocking for techs and install crew, supplier relationship management (LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Clopay, local distributors) | generally runs smoothly; occasional stockout on niche opener models causes reschedules

Dana Kowalski | Bookkeeper (part-time, contract) | reports to: Derek Vance | invoicing, bank reconciliation, tax prep coordination with outside CPA, expense tracking | in the office two days a week; anything needing same-day financial sign-off waits for her day

Sam Ostrander | Marketing Coordinator (part-time, freelance) | reports to: Derek Vance | Google/Local Services ads, social media posts, review-request follow-ups, website updates | works roughly 15 hours/week; Derek wants to expand this role and budget significantly, which is the center of the marketing-vs-phones disagreement inside the company

Where do decisions stall most: Almost everything routes through Carla — phones, scheduling, permits, payroll, and HR paperwork all sit with one person with no cross-trained backup. When she's out, overloaded, or pulled into paperwork, dispatch slows or breaks, and after-hours/peak-hour calls go to voicemail with no fallback coverage. Structural fixes (a second scheduler, an answering service, after-hours rotation) require Derek's budget approval, and Derek's stated priority is marketing spend rather than staffing the phones — so the fix keeps waiting behind a different bet.
