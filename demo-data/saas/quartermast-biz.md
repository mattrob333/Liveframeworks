BUSINESS DESCRIPTION — Quartermast

Website URL: https://quartermast.example
In their own words, what the business does: We're scheduling and job management software for small field-service companies — plumbers, electricians, garage door companies, that world. You dispatch your techs, track the job from "customer called" to "invoice paid," and everything lives in one place instead of a paper board and three spreadsheets. We built it because our founder's brother ran a 6-truck plumbing outfit and was running the whole business out of a whiteboard and a shared Google Calendar that kept getting double-booked.
Main products / services: Quartermast Scheduler (drag-and-drop dispatch board), Quartermast Jobs (job history, photos, notes, customer records), Quartermast Mobile (tech-facing app for the truck — job details, directions, photo upload, e-signature), basic invoicing (generate + email an invoice, no full accounting), QuickBooks Online one-way sync (jobs export as invoices).
Team size (rough): 15 people
Anything unusual worth knowing: The founder, Dana Reyes, still personally runs every single sales call — nobody else closes a deal, ever, even at 15 people and $1.8M ARR. Growth has been flat for the last two quarters and churn has started creeping up specifically in month 3-4 of a customer's life, not month 1. Nobody owns onboarding as a job; it's whichever CS person is free that week.
