=== CALL: churn exit interview | 2026-05-06 | Priya Chandran (Quartermast) + Ben Castellano (Owner, Castellano Electric) ===

BEN: Yeah, end of January. Used it through April, canceled last week.

PRIYA: What was the tipping point?

BEN: Honestly it just never became the thing we actually run the business out of. We were using it for new jobs coming in, but all our existing customers — the ones we'd done work for over the past five years — none of that ever made it in. So every time an old customer called back, somebody had to go dig through the paper files anyway. We ended up running two systems and it was more work, not less.

PRIYA: During your setup call, did anyone talk through migrating that old customer history?

BEN: Sort of? I remember someone saying we could import a spreadsheet if we had one, but we didn't have a clean spreadsheet, we had file folders. Nobody offered to help us actually build that data, it was on us, and we just never got around to it. Everyone's busy running jobs, nobody has a free afternoon to type five years of history into a new app.

PRIYA: That's really useful, and honestly it's on us — that should have been part of onboarding, not something we handed back to you as homework. Was the day-to-day scheduling itself okay?

BEN: Yeah, no complaints there, the scheduling board was fine, techs didn't hate the app. It's specifically that we never got our old jobs in there. So the new stuff was in Quartermast and the history was still in the file cabinet, and after a few months of that split I just thought, what are we even paying for.

PRIYA: If we'd sat down with you in week one or two and actually done that migration with you — not just told you it was possible — do you think that changes the outcome?

BEN: Probably, yeah. If it had felt like "this is where everything lives now" instead of "this is where new stuff goes," I don't think we'd have drifted back to old habits.

PRIYA: Anything about price or features that factored in, or was it really this?

BEN: Price was fine, we weren't complaining about the bill. It's specifically the history thing. If you fix that, honestly, we might come back.

PRIYA: I'm going to write that up exactly like you said it. Appreciate you being straight with me, Ben.
