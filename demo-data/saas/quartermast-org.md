ORG CHART — Quartermast

Dana Reyes | Founder & CEO | reports to | board of one (self) / no external board | Owns every sales call end to end — discovery through close, every deal, no exceptions. Also owns product vision and pricing. Runs point on trade association partnerships.

Kevin Marsh | Cofounder & CTO | reports to | Dana Reyes | Owns engineering roadmap, infrastructure, and technical hiring. Writes code daily alongside the team, not just managing. Final call on any architecture decision.

Aisha Okafor | Senior Engineer (Backend) | reports to | Kevin Marsh | Owns the scheduling engine and job data model — the core product. De facto owns the QuickBooks sync, which is a side project she picks up between roadmap items.

Ravi Talwar | Senior Engineer (Backend) | reports to | Kevin Marsh | Owns billing/invoicing logic and the payments pipeline. Also handles most production incidents since he's the most senior on-call.

Sam Dietrich | Engineer (Mobile) | reports to | Kevin Marsh | Owns the Quartermast Mobile app end to end — the only engineer who touches the tech-facing app full time.

Lena Vogt | Engineer (Full-stack) | reports to | Kevin Marsh | Splits time between the dispatch board UI and support-escalated bugs. No one else does bug triage, so she absorbs whatever CS flags as broken.

Theo Brandt | Engineer (Full-stack, junior) | reports to | Kevin Marsh | Newest hire, six months in. Working through onboarding-adjacent tooling like CSV import, mentored by Aisha.

Marisol Vance | Product Designer | reports to | Dana Reyes | Owns UI/UX across web and mobile. Only designer, so she's stretched across every surface and often designing and shipping in the same week with no research budget.

Priya Chandran | Head of Customer Success | reports to | Dana Reyes | First non-engineering hire (year two). Owns renewals, expansion calls, and — informally, not officially — whatever onboarding happens, since there's no dedicated onboarding role. Closest to churn data of anyone in the company.

Jordan Mackey | CS Rep | reports to | Priya Chandran | Runs onboarding calls and ongoing account check-ins for about half the customer base. No standardized onboarding checklist — improvises per account based on what the sales call notes said.

Nadia Osei | CS Rep | reports to | Priya Chandran | Same responsibilities as Jordan, other half of the account base. Also fields most inbound QuickBooks integration questions since she used to do bookkeeping.

Colby Reyner | Support Specialist | reports to | Priya Chandran | Owns the support inbox and live chat. First point of contact for bugs, escalates to Lena when it's a real defect versus a training gap.

Fatima Delgado | Marketing Manager | reports to | Dana Reyes | Owns trade association sponsorships, the website, and lead-gen content. Sole marketing hire — no budget for paid channels beyond conference sponsorships, so most spend decisions run through Dana directly.

Owen Prentice | Part-time Bookkeeper | reports to | Dana Reyes | Handles Quartermast's own books, payroll, and AR — roughly 15 hours a week, remote contractor arrangement, not involved in product or customer-facing QuickBooks work despite the name overlap.

Where do decisions stall most: Anything requiring Dana's sign-off, which given how the org is drawn is almost everything — every sales deal, every marketing spend, every design direction, every pricing change routes through her, and she's already the bottleneck on new revenue since she's the only closer. Onboarding specifically stalls because it has no single owner: Priya, Jordan, and Nadia all touch it informally, engineering (Theo, Aisha) builds import tooling reactively when CS escalates a bad case, and nobody has the authority or bandwidth to formalize a real onboarding process — it keeps losing to whatever Dana is currently asking sales or product to prioritize instead.
